Scoring rubric

If your RUBRIC.md differs, follow RUBRIC.md.
0Unrelated vendor. It cannot meet this need at all.
25Wrong category, but could partly help (for example a supermarket for a vegetables need).
50Right category, poor fit: outside the distance limit, or misses the preference.
75Right category and within the distance limit.
100Right category, within the distance limit, and matches the preference keyword.

Use the in-between values (10, 40, 60, 85…) when a pair falls between two anchors. Score from the name, address and distance only, and never look at the other rater's scores.

Save your scores as a CSV

Scores are kept in this browser as you work, but that copy can be lost if site data is cleared. Export after each session and keep the file. You can reopen a partly scored CSV here and carry on where you stopped.

Nothing you load here is uploaded anywhere. The sheet is read in your browser, and other columns are written back unchanged so Claude Code's checks pass.
